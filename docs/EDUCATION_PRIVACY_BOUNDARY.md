# Initial education/privacy boundary

Reviewed: 2026-10-04.

Initial commercial focus: adult purchasers, independent teachers/tutors, adult
educators and college/university instructors; appropriate student use at age 13+
subject to applicable requirements. Purchasers must be legally able to contract.

**Under-13 independent/direct onboarding is NOT approved by default.** Calcura
has not implemented a verifiable parental-consent workflow. Access arranged by
an authorized parent, guardian, school or institution still requires an appropriate
review/process; a teacher's involvement alone does not transfer all privacy duties
away from Calcura.

School/district use may need qualified privacy/legal review, a DPA or other agreement,
consent/authorization, records-management and procurement review. Those are not
implemented or certified here. Do not advertise COPPA, FERPA, GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA,
SOC 2 compliance, district approval or enterprise certification.

Escalate requests involving minors, institution-owned data, disputed authorization
or district procurement to the owner before onboarding or disclosing/deleting data.
This boundary does not establish legal compliance or a universal student age rule.
